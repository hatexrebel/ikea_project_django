ABOUT IKEA

A better everyday life starts at home.

IKEA is a globally recognised home furnishing brand known for combining functional design, affordability and practical solutions for everyday living.

From furniture and lighting to storage and home accessories, its products are designed to help people create comfortable and useful living spaces.

OUR VISION

To create a better everyday life for the many people.

The idea is simple: good home furnishing should be accessible to more people, not just a few.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Good design for everyday living.

Functional design, quality, sustainability, affordability and form are important ideas behind IKEA's approach to home furnishing.

The goal is to create products that are useful, attractive and practical for everyday life.

A BETTER FUTURE

Thinking about tomorrow.

Responsible use of resources and more sustainable ways of living are important parts of the future of home furnishing.
